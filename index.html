The 30-Second
Relief Ritual

Simple, fast, and completely mess-free — no timers or guesswork required.

Step 1

Warm It Between Your Palms

Add 2–3 drops to your palms and rub together for a few seconds. This gently warms the oil, helping it glide smoothly once applied.

Step 2

Massage in Circular Motions

Apply to the area using firm, circular strokes for 30–60 seconds. This encourages absorption and gets blood flowing to tired muscles.

Step 3

Target Your Trouble Spots

Spend extra time on stiff or swollen areas — shoulders, lower back, knees — wherever the day has taken its toll.

Step 4

Repeat Daily for Best Results

Use once or twice a day, morning and night. Consistency is key — most people notice a difference within the first week.
